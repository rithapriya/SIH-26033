## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
AgriLink is a digital platform that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, hotels, and bulk buyers. It introduces demand-based collective selling, allowing multiple farmers to combine their produce to fulfill large orders. The platform provides smart buyer-farmer matching, transparent pricing, and shared logistics coordination.

Innovation:

Demand-first agricultural marketplace.
Collective produce pooling from multiple farmers.
Smart matching based on product, quantity, location, and price.
FairShare price calculator for transparent transactions.
Shared transportation and collection point coordination.
Data-driven demand and market insights.

## Technical Approach

AgriLink is a web-based agricultural marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, hotels, and bulk buyers. The system uses demand-based collective selling, smart matching, transparent pricing, and shared logistics to reduce intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain.

1. System Development
    The platform will be developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the frontend, Python Flask for backend processing, and SQLite for database management. It will           provide separate interfaces for farmers and buyers.
2. Farmer and Buyer Registration
    Farmers can register, add their location, list available crops, quantities, prices, and harvest dates. Buyers can register and post their requirements, including           product  type, quantity, target price, and delivery location.
3. Demand-Based Collective Selling
    The system collects buyer requirements and identifies multiple farmers who can collectively fulfill a bulk order. This enables small farmers to access larger markets       without depending on intermediaries.
4. Smart Matching Algorithm
    A matching algorithm identifies suitable farmers based on product availability, quantity, location, price, and delivery date. The system ranks potential matches and        recommends the most suitable combination.
5. FairShare Price Calculator
    The system calculates the total order value, farmers' earnings, transportation expenses, platform charges, and consumer savings. This ensures transparent pricing and       fair distribution of payments.
6. Shared Logistics Management
    Nearby farmers' orders are grouped using location-based matching to identify common collection points and reduce transportation costs.
7. Future Enhancements
   AI-based demand forecasting, crop price prediction, route optimization, multilingual voice assistance, and integration with Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) can be implemented to improve scalability and efficiency.


## Feasibility and Viability
The project can be implemented as a web application using open-source technologies. The initial prototype will focus on registration, product listing, buyer requirements, order matching, collective orders, and price calculations.
                                    
1. Technical feasibility  ---      Can be developed using open-source technologies.
2. Economic feasibility   ---      Reduces unnecessary transportation and intermediary costs.
3. Social feasibility	   ---      Supports farmers and rural communities.
4. Scalability            ---      Can expand from one village to multiple districts.
5. Sustainability	       ---      Reduces food wastage and unnecessary transportation.
6. Accessibility          ---      Can support regional languages and simple mobile interfaces.

## Impact and Benefits
AgriLink aims to increase farmers' income, reduce consumer prices, minimize transportation expenses, improve market accessibility, and reduce agricultural wastage.

We can measure the success of our project through:
- Increase in farmers' average selling price.
- Reduction in consumer purchase price.
- Number of farmers connected to buyers.
- Number of collective orders completed.
- Reduction in transportation costs per kg.
- Percentage of produce sold without unnecessary intermediaries.
- Consumer savings generated.
